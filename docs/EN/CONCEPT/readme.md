# 🧠 **CONCEPT — Research Layer**

The **Concept** section contains research materials, draft ideas, and notes  
that help shape the architecture of **Arden Engine**.

This is a space for experiments, hypotheses, and R&D observations:  
from simple system behavior prototypes to theoretical models of the engine core.

Concept serves as a **connecting bridge** between the philosophy of the engine and its technical implementation.  
Ideas are born here, then later move into **Architecture**,  
are tested through **MVP**, and are defined in **Glossary**.

---

## 🎯 Section Purpose

The **Concept** section is intended as an open research space for:

- adding **ideas and implementation proposals for systems**;
- **discussing architectural decisions** and relationships between modules;
- collecting and analyzing **practices from existing game engines** such as Bevy, Unreal, Unity, Teardown, and others;
- **theoretical exploration** of voxels, physics, optimizations, and hybrid approaches;
- **recording hypotheses** that may later move into stable architecture.

---

## 🗂 Structure and Organization

For convenience, materials are divided by subtopics and files.  
Each topic has its own document and can be developed independently:

| File | Topic |
|------|------|
| [Concept_Overview.md](./Concept_Overview.md) | General concept and principles for organizing ideas |

---

## 🧩 Guidelines for Working with Concepts

1. Each new document should include a status header (`Document Status`)  
   to show the maturity level of the idea.
2. When discussing a concept, reference the related architecture files.
3. Notes may be written in free form — the main requirement is that the idea remains understandable.
4. Large proposals should be placed in a separate `.md` file with a short description and goals.
5. If a concept reaches a stable state, its logic is moved into **Architecture**.
